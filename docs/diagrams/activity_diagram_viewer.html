EMI FINANCE
Equated Monthly Installment
A fixed monthly payment made by the borrower to the lender on a specified date each month until the entire loan is paid off.
KYC SECURITY
Know Your Customer
The legal identity verification process where a user's Aadhaar and PAN cards are verified to ensure they are a real person and prevent fraud.
UPI PAYMENTS
Unified Payments Interface
India's instant real-time bank payment system (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm) that moves money directly between bank accounts within seconds.
NOC LEGAL
No Objection Certificate
A tamper-proof legal document generated at the end of the loan confirming that all dues are 100% paid and the borrower owes zero money.
LTV COLLATERAL
Loan-to-Value Ratio
The percentage of money you can borrow relative to the value of the asset you pledge. E.g., for ₹1,00,000 gold with 75% LTV, you can borrow ₹75,000.
Promissory Note LEGAL (SEC 4)
Section 4, Negotiable Instruments Act 1881
A legally binding digital IOU signed by the borrower stating: "I promise to pay back this exact amount on these dates." It is valid in a court of law.
Co-Borrower TRUST
Secondary Guarantor Signer
A trusted relative or guarantor who co-signs the loan and agrees to take legal responsibility if the primary borrower is unable to pay.
Fork & Join (━━━) UML NOTATION
Parallel Synchronization Bars
A Fork splits one path into multiple tasks running at the same time (e.g. doorstep inspection AND guarantor check). A Join waits until all finish.
Start (●) & Final (◉) UML NOTATION
Initial State & Completion Bullseye
A solid black circle marks the start of the loan request. A double-ring bullseye marks the successful completion with full repayment and asset release.
