Certified Field Verification

🛡️

Anti-Ghost Borrowing

Predatory microfinance often suffers from fake borrowers diverting funds. Loanzo disburses directly to verified vendor bank accounts upon invoice inspection, preventing cash embezzlement.

🔐

Anti-Identity Theft

Zero forged SIM cards or stolen PAN cards. Loanzo enforces Android BiometricPrompt hardware keys combined with government-backed DigiLocker Aadhaar e-KYC verification.

⛓️

Anti-Evergreening Lock

Borrowers cannot take out secondary loans to pay off earlier debt. Smart contract logic prevents roll-over lending without a mandatory physical asset re-appraisal by certified agents.

⚖️

Anti-Harassment & RBI Compliance

Fully adheres to RBI Circular RBI/2023-24/53. Eliminates unauthorized contact scraping. Every interaction is dispatched to an immutable Telegram Audit Ledger visible to all stakeholders.
