HUMAN TRANSFORMATION CASE STUDY

The Real India: From Predatory Trap to Thriving Business

How a generational artisan workshop in Jaipur broke free from 60% compound interest loan sharks through Loanzo's purpose-bound milestone escrow.

Chapter 01

The Predatory Debt Trap

Suresh Kumar, a master textile craftsman in Jaipur, faced an urgent seasonal purchase order. Traditional commercial banks rejected him for lack of audited balance sheets, forcing him into the hands of local unorganized moneylenders demanding 60% annualized compound interest.

60% Annual Interest Predatory Extortion No Legal Safety
Chapter 02

The Loanzo Collateral Pledge

Introduced to Loanzo, Suresh pledged ancestral gold ornaments into the certified Multi-Asset Collateral Escrow Vault at a transparent 72% LTV. The app instantly drafted a legally binding promissory note under Section 4 NI Act 1881 via DigiLocker biometric e-KYC.

72% LTV Ceiling DigiLocker e-KYC Zero Hidden Fees
Chapter 03

Certified Field Verification

Rather than an algorithmic black-box rejection, Rajesh—a certified local community agent—conducted a dignified on-site physical inspection. He verified the loom machinery and certified the yarn supplier's invoice directly in the agent cockpit.

Certified Field Agent Physical Asset Audit Instant Telegram Sync
Chapter 04

Flourishing Business & Safe Repayment

Disbursements were paid in milestone tranches directly to the fabric vendor (40% yarn, 35% dying, 25% shipping). After completing the export order, Suresh cleared his EMIs seamlessly via UPI intent. The collateral was unlocked with an automated discharge deed.
