INSTITUTIONAL ENFORCEABILITY

Statutory Legal Framework & FMEA Risk Matrix

How Loanzo operates strictly within sovereign Indian statutes to protect lender capital with 21-day fast-track summary court recovery while strictly enforcing RBI fair debt collection practices.

⚖️

Section 4, NI Act 1881

Every loan generates an unconditional Promissory Note payable on demand or fixed date, forming an indisputable legal debt instrument recognized by all Indian civil courts.

⚡

Order 37, CPC 1908

In the event of default, lenders file a Summary Suit. Unlike regular 3-5 year suits, the court enters judgment in 21 to 90 days unless the borrower demonstrates a triable defense.

📜

Section 65B Evidence Act

Digital promissory notes, biometric timestamps, and photo proofs are certified under Section 65B (Sec 63 BSA 2023), ensuring primary admissibility in judicial proceedings.

🏛️

RBI Circular 2023-24/53

Full compliance with RBI Fair Practices Code: penal charges are strictly non-compounding, capped at 2% monthly, and all collections occur without abusive harassment or shaming.
