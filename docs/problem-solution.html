Every screen, entity, and usecase in Loanzo was engineered to mathematically neutralize a real-world vulnerability in Indian microfinance. Explore the 6 core pillars below with authentic UI screenshots and code callouts.

THE REAL-WORLD PROBLEM IN INDIA

Identity Theft, Fake SIMs & Stolen Aadhaar/PAN Accounts

Unsecured digital lending apps suffer catastrophic delinquency waves due to synthetic identity fraud, stolen photocopies of Aadhaar cards, and cloned SIM cards creating ghost borrower accounts with zero repayment liability.

THE LOANZO PROTOCOL SOLUTION

Hardware BiometricPrompt & DigiLocker Aadhaar e-KYC

Loanzo enforces local cryptographic biometric authentication backed by the device's hardware KeyStore/StrongBox, combined with instant C-DAC DigiLocker KYC verification. No account can be created or accessed without live biometric authorization.

HOW THE APP ACTUALLY IMPLEMENTS IT

ui/auth/LoginScreen.kt & KycScreen.kt

Features dynamic role switching (Member, Field Agent, Master Admin), StrongBox-backed SHA-256 session tokens, and biometric fingerprint trigger. When the user taps 'Biometric Login', BiometricAuthManager binds with FragmentActivity and verifies the cryptographic signature.
