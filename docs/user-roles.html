DECENTRALIZED ECOSYSTEM PARTICIPANTS

4 Multi-Sided User Personas & How They Interact

Loanzo connects honest Indian entrepreneurs with verified peer investors, certified local doorstep agents, and institutional governors on a shared, tamper-evident ledger.

🌾

Member (Borrower)

Artisans / MSMEs

Pledges gold, vehicles, or trade invoices. Requests purpose-bound milestone tranches to purchase raw inventory with zero middleman exploitation.

  • Aadhaar DigiLocker e-KYC
  • Collateral Vault Appraisal
  • Upload Vendor Invoices
  • Instant Repay via UPI QR
💎

Peer Lender

Investors / Co-ops

Browses verified order feeds on the Social Marketplace. Evaluates physical inspection reports, funds fractional orders, and earns 12-18% APR yield.

  • Inspect Collateral Health
  • Syndicate Loan Bids
  • Direct Bank Payouts
  • Real-Time Telegram Receipts
🛵

Certified Field Agent

Local Community Verifier

Visits borrower sites with an offline-first inspection checklist. Assays gold, verifies equipment serial numbers, and uploads geo-stamped audit logs.

  • Physical Asset Inspection
  • Supplier Invoice Validation
  • GPS-Stamped Audit Trail
  • Rolling Security Bond Lock
🏛️

Master Admin

Protocol Governor

Oversees platform solvency, arbitrates dispute mediations, assigns physical vault lockers, and regulates compliance with statutory RBI directives.

  • Accredit Field Agents
  • Allocate Bank Lockers
  • Order 37 CPC Summary Legal Filing
  • Immutable Audit Governance
